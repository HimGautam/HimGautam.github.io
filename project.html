Recent work

01

Carl Zeiss AG · 2025–2026

Robot Learning for Contact-Rich Assembly

Master’s thesis research

Developed reinforcement-learning policies that use force feedback for contact-rich assembly in Isaac Lab. Compared observation and reward variants, then evaluated robustness under pose uncertainty, corrupted wrench data, assembly clearances, and unseen geometries.

Robot learning internship

Trained PPO manipulation policies with domain randomization and built a deployment workflow using Python, ROS, Docker, and FrankaPy. Integrated pose observations, policy inference, runtime logging, safety checks, and Cartesian impedance control.

  • PyTorch

  • Force feedback
  • Franka Panda

02

Independent project · 2025–2026

Robot Teleoperation and Multimodal Demonstration Collection

Integrated a 6-DoF AgileX Piper manipulator with Hugging Face LeRobot and developed a Python game-controller interface for physical robot teleoperation.

Structured demonstration episodes combine wrist and external RGB images, proprioception, gripper state, and actions. Workspace constraints support safe and repeatable data collection.

  • AgileX Piper
  • LeRobot
  • Python
  • Multimodal data
03

Challenge project · 2026

Paint Policy Challenge

Evaluated a provided ACT-style action-chunking policy trained from 35 expert demonstrations. The policy used point-cloud observations and was tested with closed-loop rollouts on seven unseen geometries.

Ran PyTorch ablations on observations, history, action horizon, execution length, and training perturbations; analyzed coverage, overspray, generalization limits, and failure modes.

  • Imitation learning
  • Action chunking
  • Point clouds
  • Ablation studies
