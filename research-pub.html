Research context

These publications grew from work in autonomous systems and robotics at IIIT Hyderabad and from undergraduate research in mobile robot navigation. My more recent work extends this foundation toward physical robot manipulation, multimodal demonstrations, force-aware policies, and sim-to-real evaluation.
